Nothing shows up after I enter my key

Check the key is the read-only API key from your pool account rather than a password or a wallet address, and that it was pasted without a trailing space. If the key is right and the screen is still empty, your account may have no active workers on the coins being checked — try Auto-detect, which scans for whichever coins you're actually mining.

Auto-detect versus Manual

Auto-detect scans the common coins with your key and shows only the ones you're active on — start here if you're not sure. Manual lets you pick coins yourself from the catalogue, and has a custom slug field for anything not listed.

A worker shows offline but it's running

The app reports what the pool reports. A worker that has just restarted, or that submits shares infrequently, can read as offline for a few minutes before the pool catches up. If it stays wrong for longer than that, compare it against the pool's own dashboard — if they disagree, email me.

I'm not getting offline notifications

Check notifications are allowed in Settings → Pool Monitor → Notifications. Background checks are also scheduled by iOS rather than by the app, so how often they run depends on battery level, Low Power Mode and how often you open the app. Opening it always refreshes immediately.

The widget isn't updating

Widgets refresh on a budget iOS controls, so the figure can lag the app by a while. Open the app to force a refresh. If the widget shows a dash, the last fetch failed — usually a connection problem or an expired key.

Which pools are supported?

This version connects to one pool only. Support for others is the obvious next step; if you'd like a particular one, email me and say which.

Does the app touch my funds?

No. It only ever reads. There is no wallet, no withdrawal, no payout function, and a read-only API key cannot move anything even if it were shared.

Privacy

Your API key is stored on your device and sent only to the pool's own API to fetch your stats. There is no Pool Monitor server, no account and no analytics. The full policy is at privacy-policy.html.
