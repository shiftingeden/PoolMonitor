How it works

Add a pool in seconds

Paste a payout address or a read-only API key, depending on the pool. That's it. No PoolMonitor account, ever.

Add your address or key

Solo pools only need your BTC address. Account pools use a read-only API key, which is stored in the iOS Keychain.

Follow the setup guide

Every pool has its own step-by-step guide, and its tab appears only once it's set up. Mining-Dutch auto-detects the coins you mine.

Get alerted

Offline workers, back-online notices, payouts and found blocks, with quiet hours so it never wakes you.

Safety · Security · Privacy

Watch your earnings. Never touch them.

PoolMonitor is read-only by design. It has no wallet, no withdrawal function and no payout settings. Even a leaked read-only key can't move funds.

Your key or address is only ever sent to the pool it belongs to, to fetch your stats.

Support

Questions? I answer every email.

Email TechBitsMix@gmail.com for help, bug reports or feature requests.

I entered my key but the screen is empty.

Check that you used the read-only API key from your pool account, not a password or wallet address, and that no trailing space was pasted. Then try Auto-detect: your account may have no active workers on the coins being checked.

A worker shows offline but it's running.

PoolMonitor shows what the pool reports. A worker that just restarted or submits shares rarely can read as offline for a few minutes. If it persists, compare with the pool's own dashboard and email me if they disagree.

I'm not getting offline notifications.

Check notification permissions in iOS Settings. Background checks are scheduled by iOS, so timing depends on battery, Low Power Mode and how often you open the app. Opening the app refreshes immediately.

Can PoolMonitor move or withdraw my funds?

No. The app is read-only. It has no wallet, withdrawal or payout function, and a read-only key can't move funds.

Where is my API key stored?

In the iOS Keychain on your device. It's sent only to the pool's own API to fetch your stats. There's no PoolMonitor server, account or analytics. See the Privacy Policy.

Can you add my pool?

Email me the pool name. New pools are prioritised by how many miners use them.
